About the Project

Problem: Canteens often prepare food using estimates, which leads to avoidable food waste and financial loss.

Solution: CherryBite records preparation, sales and confirmed waste, shows the analysis, and suggests how much to prepare next.

Technology: HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Records are saved in your own browser (localStorage); there is no server or database in this version.
